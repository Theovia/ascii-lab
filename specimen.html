Specimen Girl with a Pearl Earring Johannes Vermeer · c. 1665
Method Brightness ramp · 42 glyphs Cursor reveals Sobel edge plate
Render WebGL2 · 4-px cell — cells · 60 fps

The cursor passes through the painting
like an X-ray plate.

Outside cursor
The painting is rendered at full color and brightness — each cell takes the color of the underlying pixel and a glyph chosen by luminance from a forty-two-step ramp.
Inside cursor
A Sobel filter samples four neighbors per cell. The gradient direction is quantized into four bins and the corresponding stroke — / \ | _ — replaces the brightness glyph. The painting becomes line.
Color
Outside: polychrome, sampling the original palette. Inside: monochrome paper, like a study sketch under raking light.
Movement
No deformation. The image holds. Only the disposition of the glyphs changes — character substitution, not pixel pushing.
Atlas
A 46-glyph monospaced font atlas, pre-rasterized once and uploaded as an 8×6 GPU texture. Brightness ramp: 42 chars. Edge ramp: 4 chars (/ \ | _) at fixed indices.
Performance
Single-pass fragment shader. Five texture samples per fragment (one main + four Sobel neighbors). Clamped to two-pixel device-pixel-ratio. Sixty hertz at 4K.
